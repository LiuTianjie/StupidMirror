StupidMirror/Codex plugin

CODEX PLUGIN

A real phone, inside your Codex chat.

StupidMirror bundles a Codex plugin. Your agent sees the live screen of a real iPhone or Android device, operates it through MCP, and you point at exactly what you mean with annotations.

Connect in four steps

No repository clone, Python or Node.js install, or token copying is needed for the installed app.

01

Install and connect the phone

Move StupidMirror to Applications and open it. Connect and unlock the phone. USB video needs trust and Camera permission; Android needs authorized ADB.

02

Run iPhone setup once

For iPhone control or wireless video, complete Set Up This iPhone over USB.

03

Click Connect Codex

In Settings → MCP Automation, click Connect Codex. The app enables local MCP and installs its bundled plugin through the Codex desktop app's CLI.

04

Ask Codex

Open a new Codex chat, select StupidMirror and ask: “Show my phone inside Codex without tapping anything.” The panel opens through show_screen.

View, operate, annotate

01

View

Choose a device; pause and resume, refresh, return Home or copy a screenshot. Video plays in the chat without a separate mirror window.

02

Operate

Explicitly enable control, then click to tap or drag to swipe. Nothing is sent to the phone until you turn it on.

03

Annotate

Freeze the frame and draw up to eight numbered boxes, then send the marked image and your note with Command-Enter. Drawing never taps the phone.

04

Let the agent act

The agent works in an observe → act → observe loop with local OCR, element IDs and targeted taps, and checks the result on the device.

Video stays on this Mac

Hardware H.264 travels over encrypted local WebRTC, with no cloud relay or STUN/TURN service. Screenshots your agent requests and annotations you send go to your AI client and follow its data settings. Never paste the MCP connection file into a chat.

Troubleshooting

Codex shows no StupidMirror tools

Reconnect the plugin and select it in a new Codex chat. After updating the app, click Connect Codex (Update or reinstall plugin) again.

Initialization fails with -32603

Older builds reject experimental client capabilities. Update to StupidMirror 0.3.1 or later and reconnect the plugin; restarting Codex does not fix it.

No device or no video

Unlock the phone, check the cable and trust prompt or ADB authorization, and refresh. USB iPhone video needs Camera permission; wireless video needs the iPhone setup guide.

Taps do nothing

Wait for control setup, check Developer Mode and the signed runner on the iPhone, then enable Operate again. Viewing and control are ready independently.

Annotations cannot be sent

The host has to support image messages. If the panel reports an unavailable composer, open the chat, ask Codex to call show_screen again and continue in the new panel. Your draft is kept.
